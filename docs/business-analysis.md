# Business Analysis — Industrial Incident Intelligence

## 1. Business Context & Problem Statement
Industrial safety management relies on tracking workplace incidents, near misses, and occupational hazards. Organizations often collect these logs across disparate formats, creating a barrier to effective risk evaluation and timely preventive decision-making. 

This project establishes a structured analytical framework to ingest, validate, and analyze historical safety incident data, transforming raw logs into interpretable intelligence for safety managers and operational analysts.

## 2. Target Users & Stakeholders
- **Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Analysts**: Responsible for reviewing incident trends, identifying recurring risk factors, and proposing safety protocol adjustments.
- **Data Engineers & Developers**: Responsible for maintaining reproducible data pipelines, database integrity, and API performance.
- **Academic Evaluators**: Reviewing adherence to software engineering standards, data governance, and rigorous machine learning methodologies.

## 3. Decision Support Goals
- Identify historical operational risk patterns across sectors and incident categories.
- Support data-driven evaluations of incident severity levels using transparent, reproducible models.
- Provide interactive visualizations to communicate safety insights clearly to authorized personnel.

## 4. Scope and Operational Boundaries
- **In Scope**: Exploratory data analysis, data quality auditing, severity classification modeling, REST API querying, and an interactive frontend dashboard.
- **Out of Scope**: Real-time IoT sensor telemetry streaming, automated emergency response dispatches, and absolute legal compliance guarantees. Model findings serve as decision support tools and do not replace professional safety inspections.
